# AtlasLabs-HR-Analytics-Dashboard
A Power BI dashboard analysing employee demographics, performance and attrition at AtlasLabs.

This project was completed as part of a Datacamp course. 

## Overview 
The report has four pages: 
* **Overview**: Shows the total, active and inactive employees, attrition rate, employees by department and job role, hiring trends.
* **Demographics**: shows age, gender, marital status and ethnicity breakdowns, plus average salary by ethnicity.
* **Performance Tracker**: Presents per-employee satisfaction and rating trends over time, with review dates.
* **Attrition**: presents attritions by hire year, overtime, travel frequency, tenure, department and job role.

## Key Findings 
* 1.47k employees in total, 1.23k active and 237 inactive, giving a 16.1% attrition rate.
* Technology is the largest department with active employees. 
* Attrition is much higher for employees who work overtime and for frequent travellers. 
* Attrition is highest in the first two years of tenure. 
* Sales Representatives and HR Recruiters have the highest attrition by role.
